## About Me

I’m Sakib Al H., a Product Design Engineer and HCI-focused designer working across UX research, interaction design, visual systems, and frontend implementation.

At [WolfDevs](https://wolfdevs.com/), I lead UX research and end-to-end product design across WordPress, FinTech, eCommerce, Chrome extensions, web builders, and SaaS products. I work with stakeholders and software engineers to understand problems, structure information, design flows, prototype solutions, build design systems, and deliver production-ready interfaces.

My approach starts with the problem rather than the screen. I try to understand what people need, where they struggle, what they expect, and how a product can help them act with more confidence.

I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering and am preparing for master’s study in Human–Computer Interaction. My research interests include UX research, interaction design, human-centered AI, physical–digital interaction, usability, accessibility, trust, and explainability.
